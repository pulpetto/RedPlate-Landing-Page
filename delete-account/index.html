RedPlate — Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your RedPlate account and associated workout data.

Option 1: Open RedPlate → Settings → Delete Account.

Option 2: If you no longer have access to the app, email support@redplate.app with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and the email associated with your account.

We'll verify account ownership and process your deletion request. Some records may be retained where legally required.
